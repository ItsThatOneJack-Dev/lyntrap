# Contributing

Lyntrap is made better by contributions, and I (ItsThatOneJack) aim to make the process of contributing to Lyntrap as easy and simple as saying the word "simple" (well, not quite THAT simple)!

So, here is the process you should follow when trying to contribute to Lyntrap.

## The Process

1. [Fork Lyntrap](https://github.com/ItsThatOneJack-Dev/lyntrap/fork)
2. In your fork of Lyntrap, make the changes you plan to commit.
3. Commit your changes to your fork.
4. [Create a new PR](https://github.com/ItsThatOneJack-Dev/lyntrap/pulls) (Pull Request) from your fork to Lyntrap's `main` branch.
5. Wait for code analysis to go through (CodeQL will evaluate your code for potential errors, security issues, and other threats).
6. Wait for a "qualified" individual to approve your changes and eventually merge them.
7. There is no step 7, because you are done!

Please try to stay available until your PR is merged and then closed, as your PR will need to be scrutinised by others, and they may need to talk to you for various reasons, such as to suggest changes to your PR, alert you of issues with it, etc.
